# Problem 10 — Style the Website

The Problem 10 design updates give Campus Customs a recognizable, friendly, and polished Yale-inspired identity while keeping the shop easy to use.

| Change requested and made | Customer benefit |
|---|---|
| Added varsity-style typography with cream, navy, and gold colors. | Creates a cohesive school-spirit look that feels trustworthy and makes headings and navigation easy to recognize. |
| Added a large pawprint logo, oversized faint pawprints across the header, and a custom paw cursor. | Gives shoppers clear visual branding and makes browsing feel playful and memorable. |
| Added a blue glitter sparkle trail that follows mouse movement. | Provides delightful interaction feedback without blocking clicks; it is disabled for reduced-motion preferences. |
| Created a CSS-native cartoon Handsome Dan-inspired mascot with connected ears, expressive details, a navy bandana, and a proper blue backpack worn behind his body with straps, handle, and pocket. | Makes the store feel welcoming and distinctive while reinforcing the campus mascot theme. |
| Gave the chat a friendly bulldog voice and added dog-themed header details. | Makes the shopping assistant feel approachable and consistent with the Campus Customs brand. |
| Added extra header spacing, responsive mascot sizing, readable contrast, focus states, and reduced-motion support. | Prevents visual overlap and keeps the experience comfortable and accessible on desktop, mobile, keyboard navigation, and motion-sensitive settings. |

All artwork is CSS/HTML-native, so it loads with the site and does not require a separate image asset.
